Time to put the whole module together: several `print()` lines in the right order, empty lines, exact text and a comment.

You'll build a name badge, the kind people stick on at a meetup. Getting output to match *exactly* is a real programming skill. Take it one line at a time, and press Run often to compare your output with the example.

Two things to watch:

- **Spaces count.** The two spaces at the start of the middle lines go inside the quotes: `print("  Sam")`.
- **Count the `=` signs.** The top and bottom lines both have exactly 20.

Once the checks pass, feel free to put your own name on the card. It's yours.
